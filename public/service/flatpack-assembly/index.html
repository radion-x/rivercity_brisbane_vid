Flatpack furniture assembly in Brisbane

Flatpack furniture assembly turns boxed furniture into usable pieces, with the supplied parts checked and assembled according to the instructions. Share the product links, item quantities and room access details for a useful estimate.
Request a free quote

Furniture setup without the unfinished boxes

A move, a new bedroom or a home office can leave several boxes waiting for assembly. River City Handyman can organise a booking around the actual products. Send links or model names for every item and their quantities, instead of estimating the job from the number of cartons.

Beds, desks, drawers, wardrobes and shelving have different assembly requirements. Include moving parts, wall restraints and large panels. Tell us whether the items are new or partly assembled, and whether old furniture needs moving before the new pieces can be built.

Delivery and workspace come first

Confirm the furniture has arrived before arranging assembly. Some products arrive in several packages, with hardware packed separately. Check the delivery list and keep every bag of fittings with the instructions. Photograph damage and contact the supplier about missing pieces before assuming the job can go ahead.

Place cartons in the final room where possible. A completed wardrobe or bed may not fit through a door or down a staircase. Clear space for panels to be laid out and leave access to the surrounding area. Mention stairs, narrow passages and apartment lift bookings in your enquiry.

Check the installation requirements

Follow the manufacturer requirements for safe use, including wall attachment where specified. Mention tall furniture and supplied restraints. Suitable fixings depend on the wall surface, and permission may be needed before drilling in a rental or strata property. Agree the wall work as part of the scope.

Assembly is different from altering cabinetry or installing fitted joinery. If you need a piece cut down, built into a space or attached in an unusual way, explain that separately. We assess the request before treating a custom alteration as an ordinary flatpack booking.

Make a grouped booking work for your household

Put the important items first, especially if a bed or desk needs to be ready by a particular date. Send the complete list so the estimate covers the job. Several small pieces may be convenient to build together, but a large wardrobe or storage system changes the time required.

If an earlier assembly attempt has gone wrong, stop and photograph the affected parts. Incorrect screws or damaged connections may need replacements. We need to see the condition before promising completion. Packaging removal, dismantling old furniture and moving heavy items should be discussed beforehand rather than assumed to be included.

Questions about flatpack furniture assembly

Do you assemble IKEA furniture?

Ask about the specific IKEA product and send its link or model name. Include the quantity and required wall restraints.

Can you assemble several pieces in one booking?

Yes, multiple items can be discussed together. A complete product list helps us estimate the time and prioritise the work.

Will you remove the packaging?

Tell us about packaging disposal when requesting the quote. Do not assume off-site removal is included in an assembly booking.
