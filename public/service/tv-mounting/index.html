TV wall mounting in Brisbane

TV wall mounting secures your television at a practical viewing height using a bracket and fixings suited to the wall. Send the screen size, bracket details and a photo of the proposed position before booking.

A wall-mounted screen that works for the room

Before choosing a mounting point, look at the room from the seat you use most. Window glare, ceiling fans, doorways and a nearby console influence where the screen works best. River City Handyman can assess the intended position and discuss a practical arrangement before drilling begins.

Send the television model, screen size and a photograph of the room. Tell us if this is a new installation, a move from another wall or replacement of an existing screen. An existing bracket needs checking too; a previous installation does not establish that it suits the replacement television.

Match the bracket to the screen and wall

A fixed bracket sits close to the wall, a tilting bracket allows some adjustment, and a full-motion arm changes the viewing direction. The right choice depends on how you use the room and support available in the wall. Include a product link if you already have a bracket.

Wall materials vary across Brisbane homes and apartments. Plasterboard and masonry require different fixing arrangements, while unusual linings or uncertain wall condition need closer assessment. We discuss the installation based on the actual surface and fixing points rather than assuming one method works everywhere.

Settle the cable plan at the quoting stage

Photograph the existing socket and equipment such as a soundbar, console or set-top box. Cable lengths and connection points affect whether the finished installation looks tidy. If you want surface cable covers, include that request so it can be assessed with the mounting work.

Hiding cables is not automatically included in a standard mounting appointment. Wall access and services involved need checking. A new socket or changes to fixed electrical wiring require a licensed electrician. Discuss those tasks early so mounting does not leave an unresolved electrical requirement.

Brisbane booking and access checklist

Tell us your suburb, property type and access arrangements. For inner-city apartments, include lift access, loading restrictions and building rules for drilling. In a rental, organise permission for wall alterations before confirming the work. For a detached home, mention stairs and the route to the room.

Leave a clear space beneath the mounting position and keep the screen, bracket and hardware ready for inspection. Large screens may need particular handling arrangements. Include related work, such as assembling a TV unit or hanging a shelf, in the original list rather than adding it after the visit has been timed.

Questions about tv wall mounting

Can I supply my own TV bracket?

Yes. Send its model or product link before booking so compatibility with the television and intended wall can be discussed.

Is a new power point included?

No. A new or relocated power point requires a licensed electrician and a separate scope of work.

Can you remount a television in a different room?

Ask for removal and remounting in your enquiry. Send photos of both positions and mention any patching needed at the old location.
