A shelf installation needs more information than its width and preferred height. The intended contents, shelf system, supporting wall and permission to drill all affect the booking. Decide what the shelf will hold, then send the product details and a wide photograph of the proposed position.

Shelving is included in River City Handyman's [Brisbane services](/#services). This guide helps you prepare that enquiry. It is not a universal fixing recipe: a bracket or anchor suitable for one wall and product should not be assumed suitable for another.

## Begin with what will go on the shelf

A display shelf holding a few light objects presents a different question from storage for a row of books. Tell us about the intended contents rather than describing every shelf as light duty. Include the number of shelves, their depth and any manufacturer information about permitted use.

Do not assume an advertised maximum load is a guarantee for your wall. [IKEA's wall-shelf anchoring guide](https://www.ikea.com/au/en/files/pdf/54/c8/54c894df/wall_shelves_aug21_fy22.pdf) explains that different wall materials require different fixing devices. Product instructions and the actual support available must be considered together. No general load figure in a blog can replace that assessment.

## Share the complete shelf system

Send a product link or clear photographs of the shelf, brackets, rails and supplied fittings. Explain whether the items are new, previously installed or missing components. A timber board bought separately from its brackets needs a different discussion from a complete manufacturer system.

If the product needs assembly before mounting, mention that too. Our [furniture assembly service](/service/flatpack-assembly/) handles a related but distinct task. Agree whether the booking covers assembling the item, attaching it to the wall or both; neither should be silently added to the other.

## Show the surrounding wall and room

Take a wide photo showing corners, doorways, furniture and the floor beneath the planned shelf. Add a close-up if you know about old fixing holes, damage or an unusual surface. Say what you know about the wall, but identify uncertainty honestly rather than guessing from its paint finish.

A wall can contain services that are not apparent in a photograph. Do not start drilling or select fixing points merely because the position looks clear. If there is existing damage, moisture or an uncertain lining, flag it before the appointment so mounting is not assumed to be straightforward.

## Choose a position that works in everyday use

Think about reach, nearby doors and the space needed for the objects being stored. A shelf over a desk should leave useful working space; one near a doorway should not obstruct normal movement. Mark your preferred approximate position in the enquiry rather than expecting an installer to guess it.

You can use a paper outline to help visualise the size without making holes. Explain whether alignment with another shelf, picture or cabinet matters. If the existing furniture will be moved later, mention that change before agreeing the final position.

## Check permission before confirming wall work

In a rental, organise the relevant consent process before drilling. The [RTA fixture and structural-change factsheet](https://www.rta.qld.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-04/Fact-sheet-fixture-and-structural-changes-in-rental-properties.pdf) explains Queensland's process. Building or body-corporate requirements may also need checking depending on the property and proposed work.

Keep the agreed removal and making-good requirements with the approval. A future move-out may involve removing the shelf and repairing holes, so discuss that responsibility now rather than assuming an installation can be reversed without visible marks.

## Use this short enquiry checklist

| Detail | What to send |
|---|---|
| Product | Link, dimensions, quantity and bracket information |
| Use | Intended contents and available product load guidance |
| Wall | Wide photo, known material and any uncertainty or damage |
| Position | Approximate height and alignment preferences |
| Property | Suburb, access, permissions and work-hour restrictions |
