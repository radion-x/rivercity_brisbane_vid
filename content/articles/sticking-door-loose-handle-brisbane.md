For a sticking internal door or loose handle, a useful repair enquiry shows the complete opening as well as the faulty part. Explain how the door behaves during ordinary use, when it changed and whether an earlier adjustment has been attempted. You can report those details without taking the fitting apart.

River City Handyman lists general repairs and hardware work among its [Brisbane handyman services](/#services). We assess the actual request before confirming a visit. A squeak, a loose lever and a door catching on new flooring should not all be booked as the same assumed fix.

## Start with the door's job

Say which room the door serves and whether it is an internal room door, an exterior door or an apartment entry door. Include the number of doors affected. If the problem affects access, security or safe exit, make that clear immediately rather than placing it at the bottom of an ordinary maintenance list.

This article concerns preparing a routine repair enquiry. It does not prescribe work on fire doors, security systems or emergency exit hardware. A door connected with building fire safety needs the appropriate assessment and authorisation, even if the visible fault looks like a simple loose fitting.

## Give one clear description of normal use

Describe the first point where the door becomes difficult to use. For example, explain whether it catches before reaching the frame, closes but does not stay latched, or opens only when the handle is held at an unusual angle. These are hypothetical descriptions, not diagnoses.

Avoid forcing the door or repeatedly testing a fitting that may leave someone trapped. If it has already stopped working normally, report that fact and ask for the suitable repair route. A photograph of the handle alone cannot show the clearance around the leaf or the condition of the frame.

## Gather a small photo set

Take a wide photograph of the opening, a view of the open door showing the hinge side and a close-up of the area involved. For a loose lever, show both faces of the handle and the latch on the door edge. Keep the surroundings visible enough to show access and nearby furniture.

| What you notice | What makes the photo set useful |
|---|---|
| An edge catches | Show the contact area and the whole frame |
| The door will not stay shut | Show the latch and the receiving plate |
| A handle is loose | Show both sides, plus any visible missing part |
| The fault followed other work | Describe the work and when the change appeared |

Do not remove hardware just to create a clearer picture. Loose parts, damaged screw holes and mismatched replacement fittings can change the scope, so mention anything you already know about them.

## Separate parts from labour

If you want a handle replaced, explain whether the replacement is already purchased. Share the product link, finish and quantity, then let compatibility be checked against the existing door. A replacement that looks similar may still require a different preparation or additional work.

Ask whether parts collection, making good old fixing holes, paint touch-ups and disposal are included. There is no fixed price or universal visit length in this guide. The number of doors, fault, parts, access and agreed finish determine what needs to be quoted.

## Confirm the correct permission and repair route

Tenants should discuss the reported fault and approved work with the agent or property manager before arranging non-urgent alterations. The [Queensland RTA repairs information](https://www.rta.qld.gov.au/repairs) is a starting point for understanding the repair process; the circumstances of an individual tenancy still matter.

For a unit entry door or shared building area, contact the body corporate or building manager. [Queensland Fire Department guidance for building owners and occupiers](https://www.fire.qld.gov.au/compliance-and-planning/building-owners-and-occupiers) covers building fire-safety responsibilities. Do not remove closers or alter potentially fire-rated doors as an ordinary DIY solution.

## Include access and related jobs

Send your Brisbane suburb, stairs or lift arrangements, parking options and any building restrictions. Clear the route to the affected opening before an agreed appointment, but leave the faulty fitting in place unless specifically advised otherwise.
