A cupboard door that sits unevenly may need an adjustment, a replacement hinge or repair to the material supporting its fittings. Start by describing the symptom and photographing the door, cabinet and hinge. Avoid assuming that tightening one screw will solve every door that sags or will not close.

River City Handyman's [Brisbane general-repair services](/#services) cover household hardware enquiries. This guide helps you prepare an assessment request for existing cupboards. It is separate from planning new cabinetry, a kitchen renovation or assembling a boxed piece of furniture.

## Describe the fault during ordinary use

Explain whether the door rubs against its neighbour, sits low at one corner, will not stay closed or closes with a gap. For a soft-close fitting, say what has changed: the door might close too quickly, stop before reaching the cabinet or behave differently from the adjacent door.

Note the number of affected doors and whether the problem started suddenly or developed over time. Include previous repair attempts and any known impact. A clear symptom description is more useful than naming a replacement part without checking what is already installed.

If a hinge is pulling away or the door appears likely to fall, stop using the affected cupboard and keep people clear until it can be assessed. Do not repeatedly open it to film the fault, or remove fittings that may still be supporting the door.

## Photograph the whole cabinet and its fittings

Send a wide view with the doors closed, plus an open-door view if it can be obtained safely. Include each affected hinge and the area where it attaches to both the door and cabinet. A close-up of a hinge alone can omit the damaged material around it.

Look for a visible maker's name or part code without dismantling anything. Product information can help identify the fitting, but a brand name alone does not establish a matching replacement. Different opening angles, door positions and mounting plates can matter. Send an existing product link or receipt if you have one.

| What you notice | Useful supporting detail |
|---|---|
| Uneven gaps | Closed-door photo showing adjacent doors |
| Hinge or plate moves | Close-up of attachment points and surrounding material |
| Soft-close action changed | Hinge identification and a plain symptom description |
| Panel is swollen or broken | Wide and close photos, plus any known moisture history |

## Adjustment depends on the hinge system

Some concealed hinges allow alignment in several directions. [Blum's CLIP top BLUMOTION guidance](https://www.blum.com/au/en/products/hingesystems/clip-top-blumotion/assembly/) describes side, height and depth adjustment for that system. It does not mean every cupboard uses the same screws, clip mechanism or adjustment range.

An alignment adjustment and repairing a failed attachment point are different tasks. If screws have lost their hold in damaged material, adjusting the hinge may leave the supporting problem untouched. This guide does not provide a universal screw-turning sequence or instruct you to release a hinge while the door is unsupported.

For an item still under warranty, check the supplier's process before arranging alterations. Keep the product information and photographs so you can explain the fault without guessing its cause.

## Discuss damaged panels before buying new hinges

Mention swelling, crumbling material, a split door or marks suggesting ongoing moisture. A new fitting does not resolve the source of water damage, and a repair may be unsuitable if the panel cannot support the hardware. Plumbing or other specialist work may need to be arranged separately where an active problem is involved.

Ask which outcome is being assessed: restoring ordinary operation, replacing hardware, repairing attachment points or replacing a panel. Matching an older door's finish or sourcing discontinued hardware may need a separate discussion. No single visit length, price or promise of an invisible repair applies to every cupboard.

## Keep parts and finishing work explicit

If you have already bought a hinge or handle, send the link and quantity before the appointment. Leave the original pieces available for comparison. Similar-looking parts may not match the existing holes or door position, and changing to a different system can add work.

Agree whether materials sourcing, removal, making good old holes and finish touch-ups are included. Clear ordinary stored items from the work area once a safe appointment plan is agreed. Keep cleaning products or fragile objects away from the immediate workspace.

## Add rental permission and related repairs

Tenants should report the fault and agree the repair arrangements with the agent or property manager before arranging non-urgent work. The [Queensland RTA repairs information](https://www.rta.qld.gov.au/repairs) explains where to find the relevant process. A repair to an existing fitting and a requested upgrade should be identified separately.
