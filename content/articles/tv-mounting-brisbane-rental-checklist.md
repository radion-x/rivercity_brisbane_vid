Before arranging TV mounting in a Brisbane rental, confirm the permission process and any conditions for installing and later removing the fixture. Then send the TV model, bracket information, wall photos and access details so the installation can be assessed.

A quote and permission serve different purposes. Our [Brisbane TV mounting service](/service/tv-mounting/) can discuss the proposed work, but that discussion does not replace approval from the relevant property parties.

## Start with Queensland rental guidance

The [Queensland Residential Tenancies Authority fixture-change fact sheet](https://www.rta.qld.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-04/Fact-sheet-fixture-and-structural-changes-in-rental-properties.pdf) explains the request and approval process, including Form 23. It also addresses properties where body corporate approval may be needed.

Read the current guidance and speak with the property manager about your proposed installation. Keep written approval and conditions, including any arrangements for removal or making good the wall. If a point is unclear, resolve it with the relevant party before booking work that depends on it.

This is a booking guide, not advice about an individual tenancy dispute. Do not assume a previous tenant's installation or an existing hole gives permission for your proposed changes.

## Describe the whole installation

State whether you need a new bracket fitted, an old bracket removed, a TV replaced or an accessory added. Include the TV and bracket model numbers, product links and supplied instructions.

Ask the installer to check compatibility for the actual products. A larger screen may change the job, but screen size alone is not enough to assess the mount. Do not assume the bracket left by a previous tenant is suitable or correctly installed.

## Photograph the wall and nearby outlets

Provide a photo of the full wall, a closer image of the proposed position and a view of existing outlets. Mention previous damage or an installation already present.

Say whether the wall type is known and supply any relevant building information. The installer must assess the construction and product requirements before choosing an approach. Do not open or drill the wall to provide that information yourself.

For an apartment, include any restrictions on penetrations or work hours that you have been given. For any other property type, describe the actual wall and access rather than relying on assumptions about the age or style of the home.

## Separate mounting from power and cable work

Explain the result you want: a bracket installation, a neater visible cable arrangement or additional work behind the screen. Ask what is included and who is qualified to handle any work requiring a licensed professional.

Moving a power point is not part of an ordinary bracket-fitting request. Regulated electrical or cabling work needs separate confirmation with the appropriate professional. Avoid buying concealment products before their suitability and permitted installation method have been checked.

If you are keeping the existing outlet locations, show them in the enquiry. The installer can then discuss the proposed scope without assuming changes you have not requested or obtained permission for.

## Plan access and the appointment

Include parking, loading access, stairs, lift arrangements and the room where the work will take place. Confirm permitted hours with the property manager or building manager where applicable.

Tell us if another person must attend or approve the final position. Share your preferred date and any move-in deadline, then confirm availability. A booking estimate should reflect the actual wall, products and work rather than a universal installation time.

## Keep the removal requirements with your records

If the approval specifies restoration or removal, retain that information with the bracket instructions and installation details. Photos of the wall before work begins can help document its condition.

Do not assume a small touch-up will satisfy every agreed removal condition. Discuss the required result when the fixture is being approved, and revisit it before making changes at the end of the tenancy.

## Send this information for a quote

- Suburb, property type and intended installation room
- TV and bracket model numbers or product links
- Wall and outlet photos
- Written approval and relevant conditions
- Accessories, existing bracket removal and cable preferences
- Parking, stairs, lift arrangements and permitted hours
